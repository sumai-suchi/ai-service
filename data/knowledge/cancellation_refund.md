# ParcelPilot Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Cancellation Rules
- Shipments can be cancelled free of charge if the status is PENDING_APPROVAL or CREATED.
- Once a courier is assigned or the parcel is PICKED_UP, cancellation cannot be done online. Customers must contact support.

## Refund Timeline
- If eligible, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days back to the original payment method (bKash/Nagad/Card).
- Delivery charges are non-refundable once the parcel has been picked up.

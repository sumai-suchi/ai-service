# ParcelPilot Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Delivery Timelines
- Inside Dhaka: Standard delivery takes 24 to 48 hours. Express delivery takes 12 to 24 hours.
- Outside Dhaka / Inter-district: Delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Delivery Charges
- Inside Dhaka: 60 BDT for up to 1 kg. 15 BDT for each additional kg.
- Outside Dhaka: 120 BDT for up to 1 kg. 25 BDT for each additional kg.
- Express delivery incurs an additional 50 BDT surcharge.

## Delivery Attempts
- ParcelPilot couriers will attempt delivery a maximum of 3 times before initiating Return to Sender (RTS).
- Customers will receive an OTP via SMS which must be shared with the courier to complete delivery.
